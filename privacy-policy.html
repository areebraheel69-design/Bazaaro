Velora Privacy Policy

Effective date: August 20, 2026

Velora is a personal money-tracking application for recording income, expenses, budgets, savings goals and financial activity.

Privacy at a glance: Velora is designed to keep your financial information on your device. The app does not operate a Velora cloud account or server for storing your transactions.

1. Information stored by the app

Depending on the features you use, Velora may store information you enter, including your display name, email address, profile information, income and expense transactions, categories, budgets, savings goals, avatar/preferences and app settings. This information is stored locally on your device/browser storage and is used to provide the app's features.

2. Financial information

Transaction amounts, descriptions, categories, budgets and goals are used to calculate and display balances, spending trends, analytics and progress inside Velora.

3. Privacy Lock

Velora includes an optional four-digit Privacy Lock intended to reduce casual access to in-app financial screens. It is not a replacement for operating-system security or device encryption.

4. Notifications

If you enable reminders and grant notification permission, Velora may use your device's notification capability to display reminders or alerts. Permission is controlled by your operating system/browser.

5. Third-party services

Velora may load fonts from Google Fonts. Your device may communicate with Google's font infrastructure when those resources are requested. This is subject to Google's policies.

6. Data sharing

We do not sell, rent or trade your financial information. Velora does not routinely receive or share your transaction records with third parties.

7. Data deletion

You can delete locally stored Velora data using the app's reset/delete function or by clearing the app/browser storage. Uninstalling the app may also remove local data depending on your device.

8. Security

Velora uses local device storage and provides an optional privacy lock. No electronic storage method is completely secure, so use your device's built-in screen lock and keep your operating system updated.

9. Children's privacy

Velora is a general personal-finance utility and is not specifically directed at children. We do not knowingly collect children's personal information through a Velora server.

10. Changes

We may update this Privacy Policy when the app's features or data practices change. The updated version will show a new effective date.

11. Contact

For privacy questions about Velora, contact the developer through the app's official store/developer page.
